## What Is Bulk SMS?

Bulk SMS is a messaging service that allows businesses to distribute text messages to a large number of recipients at the same time. Instead of sending messages individually, organizations can manage their communication through a centralized platform.

Businesses commonly use bulk messaging for:

- Promotional offers and discounts
- Product and service announcements
- OTP and account verification
- Payment and transaction notifications
- Appointment reminders
- Customer service updates

It provides a convenient way to manage regular communication with customers.

## Bulk SMS for Promotional and Transactional Communication

Businesses generally use SMS for different communication purposes.

**Promotional SMS:** Used to share discounts, special offers, product launches, and marketing announcements.

**Transactional SMS:** Used for order updates, payment confirmations, account notifications, and service-related information.

**OTP SMS:** Used to deliver one-time passwords during registration, login, and account verification.

Selecting the right message type helps organizations deliver relevant information based on the purpose of communication.
